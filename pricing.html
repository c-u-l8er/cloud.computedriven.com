Pricing

Planned packaging, published early so it can be argued with. Nothing on this page is purchasable — billing is not built, and there is no checkout to hide behind a button.

Nothing here is deployed. Of 40 capabilities, 24 run and are tested on a developer machine and 16 are written down and not built. 0 are serving the public. The full table says which is which, as of 2026-08-22.

Plans

Capacity figures are the machine-sized state under management. Prices are planned and not yet chargeable.
Plan Price Machine state WRL graph history
Free $0 10 GB unlimited
Driver $6/mo 100 GB unlimited
Fleet $19/mo 500 GB unlimited
Factory $69/mo 2 TB unlimited
Enterprise custom dedicated unlimited

Every plan includes unlimited WRL graph history — the provenance, the tracks, every attempt your agents ever made. That is not generosity: the graph is about 0.2% of a backup at its ceiling, so keeping every version of it costs us approximately nothing and there would be no honest reason to meter it.

Machine state is a different thing and is metered. Retained unique bytes — chunks and snapshots of the machine itself — count against the quota above. Under content addressing an unchanged chunk is stored once however many versions reference it, so history is far cheaper than a naive full-copy backup; it is not free. Saying otherwise would collapse the two world sizes back together, which is the one distinction this product rests on.

These plans do not include a machine

Stated plainly because the opposite assumption is easy and would be ruinous. Driver, Fleet and Factory buy persistence, sync, restore and fleet control for machines you already have. They do not include hosted CPU, memory or GPU.

The arithmetic is not close. A single permanent 16-core hosted runner costs a multiple of Factory's entire price — so a storage tier cannot absorb one without inverting its own margin. Hosted capacity will therefore be metered separately:

Those rates are not set. When they are, they will appear here with the date they were measured — not before.

Why there is no sign-up button

Because taking money for something requires it to exist. Provisioning, billing and entitlements are the last milestone in the plan, not the first, and a checkout flow in front of unbuilt capacity is just a way of borrowing from a customer.
